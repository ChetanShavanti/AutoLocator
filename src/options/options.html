AutoLocator Settings

AutoLocator analyzes the visible page locally to generate automation locators. No page content is uploaded by default.

Preferences

Permissions

  • activeTab — Analyze only the tab you invoke the extension on.
  • scripting — Inject the analyzer when you click Analyze Page.
  • storage — Save your language and locator preferences locally.

Privacy

Analysis runs in your browser. AutoLocator does not transmit page HTML, screenshots, or browsing history to external services in this MVP build.
